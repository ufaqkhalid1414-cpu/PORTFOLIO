Do / don’t

  • Keep dark forest + one mint family + Fraunces/Outfit.
  • Keep spark mark, pill CTAs, one italic word, numbered work, footer UFAQ fade.
  • Keep gradient in the five listed places only.
  • Use real projects, real contact, real screenshots.
  • Do not add a second accent color, neon, or emojis in the UI.
  • Do not put Fraunces on body copy.
  • Do not clone MORF, Onix, or Aezo identity (name, photo, fake quotes).
  • Do not fill every card with glow or gradient.
  • Do not ship placeholder grey bars where a screenshot belongs.
