Rules: one italic mint word per big headline. Section titles start with a mint arrow. Keep line-height tight on display (~1.05–1.1), 1.5 on body. Never mix a third font family.

Components

Buttons

Primary is always mint fill + dark text + pill radius. Icon buttons are forest circles with mint glyphs (download, plus, arrow). No drop shadows; glow is optional behind the primary CTA.

Service accordion

Website Development — open row with 2–3 lines of copy.

Webflow Development Web Performance Responsive Design CMS Implementation

Closed rows: circular icon + label + chevron. Swap labels to Full-stack, APIs, Databases if that matches your work.

Work card

Index 01 · Title · one-line outcome · small Contact Me. Thumbnail of the project UI. Cards sit on dark panels, not white.

Contact form

Fields are dark, low-contrast border, 12–16px radius. Submit can be a full-height mint bar on desktop.

Photo + ribbon

Portrait in a rounded rectangle (~24px radius) with a mint-to-black overlay. A curved ribbon repeats “Portfolio”. A circular “Let’s explore more” control with a down arrow sits on the photo edge.

Spacing, radius, motion

Grid8px base. Section padding ~80px desktop, ~40px mobile. Content width ~1100–1200px.
RadiusPills 999px. Cards 16–24px. Inputs 12px. Photo frame 24px.
MotionSlow ribbon drift. Soft hero glow. Accordion height. Optional device-rotate interstitial. Keep under 400ms for UI, longer only for ambient loops.
Grid overlayVery faint mint squares on the hero, like graph paper.

How to use this without copying

  • Keep dark forest + one mint accent, spark mark, pill CTAs, italic+grotesk headline pair, one-page sections.
  • Replace Morf, Italy, the hat photo, Photon Collective, and lorem skills with your name, city, photo, and real projects.
  • Map Services to what you actually do (e.g. Frontend, Backend, Databases, APIs).
  • Map Skills to your stack (HTML/CSS, JS, React, Node, SQL, Git…).
  • Keep Contact as a real form (or mailto) with real email/phone.
  • Do not ship the original portrait, MORF wordmark, or “Based on Italy”.
  • Do not keep fake testimonials or placeholder project names.
  • Do not add extra neon colors, emojis in the UI, or a second accent.
  • Do not use the purple ATURECODE sample — it is not selected.
